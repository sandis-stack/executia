How it works

  1. You choose one process — for example, supplier invoice → payment.
  2. We take your records for that process — invoices, approvals and payments for an agreed period. Your data stays in the EU/EEA under a data processing agreement.
  3. We identify concrete risks — duplicate payments, payments without approval, amounts that do not match.
  4. You get every finding with its evidence — the source document, the amount and the rule it breaks. Together we define the checks that run before the next payment.

How results are proven

  • Recovered money — proven by the bank record.
  • Prevented loss — proven by a wrong payment that was stopped.
  • Time saved — measured before and after.

Nothing is promised in advance. If we find nothing, you get that in writing too.

Good fit if

  • You pay many supplier invoices from more than one system or team.
  • Payments are approved by several people.
  • You have had a duplicate or wrong payment before or cannot rule it out.
