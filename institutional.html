PURPOSE

In high-stakes environments, checking afterwards is often too late.

Public bodies, regulated companies and enterprises need the same logic at the moment an action is approved, paid, recorded or committed.

Validation

Rules before action

Required rules, documents, roles and conditions are checked before execution proceeds.

Decision

Consistent outcomes

Every request is approved, blocked or routed to review by the same deterministic logic.

Record

Proof at the moment of decision

The decision is recorded when it is made, not reconstructed later from reports and emails.
