EXECUTIA LIFE service

EXECUTIA LIFE is a product of EXECUTIA AS, available at https://life.executia.io. LIFE is a workspace in which a user may run day-to-day business execution, including customers, projects, invoices, money, documents and next actions, subject to the features actually available in the user’s account.

LIFE may also process account, balance and transaction data obtained through Open Banking / Account Information Services after the user gives consent, as described below. LIFE does not provide payment initiation (PIS) functionality.

Open Banking / Account Information Services

Where you choose to connect a payment account to LIFE, EXECUTIA LIFE uses Open Banking Account Information Services (AIS) to retrieve account, balance and transaction data.

That data is obtained only after your consent. You authorise access at your bank (or other account-servicing payment service provider) through the AIS consent flow. LIFE does not retrieve AIS account, balance or transaction data without that consent.

Depending on what your bank provides, AIS data may include account identifiers, account holder name, IBAN or other account numbers, balances, and transactions (including dates, amounts, descriptions and counterparties where supplied).

AIS data is processed to display and use that information in your LIFE workspace so you can review accounts, balances and transactions in LIFE. It is not processed to initiate payments. EXECUTIA AS does not sell this data.

Enable Banking as AIS infrastructure provider

Enable Banking (Enable Banking Oy) is the regulated Open Banking / Account Information Services infrastructure provider used by EXECUTIA LIFE for AIS. Enable Banking is a registered Account Information Service Provider (AISP) supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA). Further information is published by Enable Banking at enablebanking.com.

Enable Banking provides the technical and regulated AIS connection between your bank and LIFE after you consent. EXECUTIA AS uses that infrastructure; EXECUTIA AS is not itself a licensed Account Information Service Provider (AISP).

You may review and terminate AIS data-sharing consents with Enable Banking at https://enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents/.
